# Build Me: AI Study Buddy

## Context
I am an adult male who sometimes struggles to focus on the task at hand.
I find it helpful to have someone else in the room to keep me accountable.
I don't notice when I lose focus.
I lose track of time when I'm focusing.

## Why
- I don't want my focus issues to hold me back from achieving my goals
- I want to complete tasks faster, in one go, without distractions
- I want to be able to focus on one task at a time

## What
- Use my webcam to watch me while I'm working
- Click a button to start a focus session
- Start a stopwatch when I click the button
- Show a face on the screen that looks at my face and smiles when I'm focused, and gets angry when I'm not focused

## How
- Use JS, HTML, and CSS to build it
- Use the webcam of the computer to watch me

# PRD

Thesis: Build an AI Study Buddy that uses your webcam to keep you focused by giving real-time, intuitive feedback.

Context
You lose focus and time without noticing. The app aims to recreate that accountability of having someone present.

Who We’re Building For
An adult who struggles with staying on task and needs external cues to stay focused.

Why We’re Building It
To remove focus issues as a barrier, enabling faster, undistracted task completion through an engaging, face-driven accountability system.

What Success Looks Like
Users start sessions with a click, and the app tracks time accurately. The on-screen face smiles when focus is maintained and shows displeasure when it isn’t. Users report better focus and productivity.

Epics and Stories
	1.	Focus Session:
 • User clicks a button to start a session and a stopwatch begins.
 • User can end the session, pausing time tracking.
	2.	Webcam Monitoring:
 • App accesses the webcam.
 • Real-time face analysis detects focus patterns.
	3.	Visual Feedback:
 • A digital face looks at the user and changes expression based on focus.
 • Smiling when focused, angry when distracted.
