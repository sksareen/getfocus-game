HOW IT WORKS

🎯 Face Detection

We use Google's BlazeFace AI model to track your face in real-time. The green box shows your face is detected with high confidence, while a yellow box means lower confidence.

The blue crosshair marks the center of your face for calibration.

📊 How Scoring Works

✅
Face detected + high confidence
Your score increases gradually (0.1-0.3 per second)
⚡
Consistency bonus
Long focus streaks (10s, 30s, 60s) give extra points
⚠️
Look away for >2 seconds
Counts as a distraction event
❌
Look away for >5 seconds
Score decreases (up to -2 points per second)

👁️ Visual Feedback

🟢
Green glow on canvas = Deep focus state
🔴
Red pulse on canvas = Distracted state
🔊
Down-twinkle sound = Focus score dropped below 60%
🎵
Up-skip sound = Milestone achieved or challenge completed

🎚️ Calibration Period

When you start a session, you'll see a 5-second calibration overlay. This gives you time to:

  • Position your face in the center
  • Adjust your distance from the camera
  • Ensure good lighting
  • Get comfortable before scoring begins

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Lighting: Make sure your face is well-lit
  • Position: Keep your face centered in the webcam view
  • Distance: Sit at a comfortable distance (arm's length)
  • Focus: The webcam opacity is low (30%) to minimize distraction
  • Audio: Use headphones for better ambient sound experience
